8.42. The “Tone Mapping” Submenu

The Tone Mapping submenu contains operations that allow you to use tone mapping to adjust the colors of images. Most of the filters provide ways to adjust High Dynamic Range images (HDR) to presentation using a low dynamic range.

8.42.1. Activating the Submenu

You can access this submenu from the main menu through Colors → Tone Mapping.
