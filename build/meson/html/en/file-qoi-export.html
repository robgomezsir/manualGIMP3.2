5.45. Export Image as Qoi

5.45.1. Overview

You can export your image in qoi format, which is short for “Quite OK Image” format, when you choose the “.qoi” extension, or explicitly choose “Quite OK Image” as file type in the export dialog.

There are no settings to choose from. No export dialog will open after you have chosen your filename. It will automatically start the export.
