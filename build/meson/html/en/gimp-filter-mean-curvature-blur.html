3.5. Mean Curvature Blur

3.5.1. Overview

Figure 17.16. Example for the “Mean Curvature Blur” filter

Original image

Filter applied


Regularize geometry at a speed proportional to the local mean curvature value: this filter uses the mean curvature algorithm to blur the image preserving edges.

The Mean Curvature algorithm is described on Wikipedia.

3.5.2. Activating the Filter

This filter is found in the main menu under Filters → Blur → Mean Curvature Blur….

3.5.3. Options

Figure 17.17. “Mean Curvature Blur” filter parameters


Presets, “Input Type”, Clipping, Blending Options, Preview, Merge filter, Split view
Note

Iterations

Increasing Iterations increases blur.
