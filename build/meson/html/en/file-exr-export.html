5.17. Export Image as OpenEXR

5.17.1. Overview

The OpenEXR image format allows you to export as a high dynamic range multi-channel raster image. You can export your image in OpenEXR format, when you choose the “.exr” extension, or explicitly choose “OpenEXR” as file type in the export dialog.

There are no settings to choose from. No export dialog will open after you have chosen your filename. It will automatically start the export.
