5.35. Export Image as OpenRaster (ORA)

5.35.1. Visão geral

You can export your image in OpenRaster format, when you choose the “.ora” extension, or explicitly choose “OpenRaster” as file type in the export dialog.

There are no settings to choose from. No export dialog will open after you have chosen your filename. It will automatically start the export.
