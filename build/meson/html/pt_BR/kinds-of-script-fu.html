2.4. Diferentes tipos de Script-Fus

Existem dois tipos de Script-Fus:

Script-Fus autônomos

These scripts do not require an existing image. They usually create an image themselves. In the past there were several scripts supplied with GIMP that belonged to this category. However, the results all looked dated compared to todays standards and they were not well maintained. Which is the reason that they are not installed anymore since GIMP 2.10.

Caso você queira continuar usando esses scripts, eles ainda estão disponíveis como downloads separados que você precisa instalar por conta própria. Os scripts e outros recursos podem ser baixados em aqui.

Script-Fus dependentes de imagem

Most scripts and plug-ins are logically categorized and added to the menu that resembles their function. Most of the scripts appear in the Filters menu, but there are also several in the Colors menu.

Some scripts with specific functions appear in other menus, e.g. the script New Brush (script-fu-paste-as-brush) is integrated in the Edit menu (Edit → Paste as → Paste as New Brush…), that is more logical.

Nota

Alguns scripts mais antigos que não foram atualizados ainda podem aparecer em um menu de nível superior dedicado Script-Fu.
