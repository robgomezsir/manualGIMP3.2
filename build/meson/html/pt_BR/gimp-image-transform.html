6.17. “Transform” Submenu

The items on the Transform submenu transform the image by flipping it or rotating it.

6.17.1. Ativando o submenu

You can access this submenu from the main menu through Image → Transform.

6.17.2. O conteúdo do submenu “Transformar”

O submenu Transformar tem os seguintes comandos:

  • Espelhar horizontalmente; Espelhar verticalmente

  • Rotate 90° clockwise / counter-clockwise; Rotate 180°; Arbitrary Rotation
