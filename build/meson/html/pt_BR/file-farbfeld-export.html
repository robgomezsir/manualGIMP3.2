5.18. Export Image as Farbfeld

5.18.1. Visão geral

You can export your image in farbfeld format, when you choose the “.ff” extension, or explicitly choose “Farbfeld” as file type in the export dialog.

There are no settings to choose from. No export dialog will open after you have chosen your filename. It will automatically start the export.
